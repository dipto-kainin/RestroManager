# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Restaurant managers, supervisors, and waitstaff. They work in a fast-paced environment where speed, efficiency, and accuracy of order/table tracking are critical. They need a system that minimizes administrative overhead, displays real-time floor statuses, and allows instant status updates.

## Product Purpose
RestroManager provides a central control panel for tables, menus, orders, and invoices in a restaurant. It aims to reduce order errors, improve table turnover, and automate billing processes, creating a smooth coordination between front-of-house staff and kitchen workers.

## Brand Personality
Vibrant, Energetic, Trustworthy. We want the dashboard to feel alive, warm, and highly usable, echoing the energy of a bustling high-end bistro without losing the clarity of a reliable dashboard tool.

## Anti-references
- **Bland default admin panels**: Slate-gray and blue corporate boxes with flat tables that feel like spreadsheets.
- **AI-purple SaaS templates**: Neon violet backgrounds with glowing components that suggest a tech startup rather than a hospitality setting.
- **Over-rounded bubble components**: Cards and inputs with 32px+ rounding that look cartoonish.

## Design Principles
1. **Physical Scene Context**: Designed for bright, high-paced environments. High contrast and clean text layout ensure readability under kitchen or dining room ambient lighting.
2. **Tactile Responsiveness**: Interactive elements respond instantly to touches or clicks with physical spring motions, confirming the system heard the user.
3. **Status Clarity**: Table statuses and order phases are color-coded with distinct vibrant hues (Teal, Amber, Orange, Berry) so that staff can scan the room/board in less than a second.
4. **Focused Actions**: UI prompts remain close to their triggers, avoiding modal jumps or deep nested dialogs where possible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 for body copy and placeholder text.
- Full support for `prefers-reduced-motion` to collapse animations to static transitions.
- Interactive elements have distinct borders or high-contrast background highlights, avoiding color-only status indicators.
